Kenya Government Services Guide

Simple steps to help you use government services.

KRA PIN

A KRA PIN is a personal tax number. You need it for work, banking and many other services

Documents you need

  • National ID
  • An email address
  • A phone number

Steps

  1. Open the official KRA iTax website.
  2. Choose the option to register as a new taxpayer.
  3. Fill in your details exactly as they appear on your ID
  4. Submit and keep your confirmation message.

Social Health Authority ( SHA )

Runs the Social Health Insurance Fund and you register to access public and private health services

Documents Needed

  • National ID - adults
  • Birth Certificates - children
  • Phone Number
  • Email address

Ways to Register

Registration is free and you can register by choosing among this 3 ways

  • USSD Code: Dial *147#, and follow the steps
  • Register online on the SHA portal
  • Visiting SHA or Huduma Branch near you

Option 1: Online

  1. Go to the official SHA website, sha.go.ke, and choose Register.
  2. Enter your National ID details.
  3. Create a PIN, upload a photo, and fill in your phone number and employment information.
  4. Add any dependents before submitting.
  5. Submit, and keep your confirmation message.

Option 2: USSD Code dialing

  1. Dial *147# on your phone.
  2. Follow the prompts and enter your National ID
  3. Keep the confirmation message you receive

Option 3: In Person

If you can't use USSD or web self-registration, visit your nearest Huduma Center for assistance.

After Registration

  • Check your status before your visit a hospital, rather than after.
  • Contributions:Lipa SHA Pole Pole - for self-employed personnel and employed at 2.75% of income deduction.
  • Paying via Mobile MoneySHA paybill, 200222. Account Number, your National ID
  • Help SHA toll-free helpline is 0800720601

Tips for you

  • Register through sha.go.ke or Dial *147# only. Nobody should ask for money to register you!
  • Spell your name exactly as it appears on your ID.
  • If registration fails, the usual cause is a phone number doesn't match your ID records. Update that first, or ask at a Huduma Centre.
