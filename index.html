KRA PIN

A KRA PIN is a personal tax number. You need it for work, banking and many other services

Documents you need

  • National ID
  • An email address
  • A phone number

Steps

  1. Open the official KRA iTax website.
  2. Choose the option to register as a new taxpayer.
  3. Fill in your details exactly as they appear on your ID
  4. Submit and keep your confirmation message.
